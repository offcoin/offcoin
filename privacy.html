Privacy Policy

OffCoin · Last updated: 3 August 2026

OffCoin rewards you for time spent away from your phone. This policy explains exactly what we store, why, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

The short version. We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. There is no advertising identifier, no analytics SDK and no third-party tracker in this app. We ask for no device permissions — not location, not contacts, not photos, not health data. What we store is listed in full below.

What we do not collect

No location. No contacts. No photos. No microphone or camera. No health or fitness data. No advertising identifier (IDFA). No browsing history. No list of apps installed on your device.

Importantly: we do not know what you do on your phone. OffCoin detects only whether the screen is locked, using an operating-system signal. It cannot see which apps you open, what you type or what you look at. That information never exists in the app, so it can never leave your device.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. Delete the account and it goes with it — see below. Rate-limit counters are removed automatically after ten minutes. Settled room records are removed after seven days; what remains is an aggregate count of how many people finished, which points to no individual.

Deleting your account

Open Profile → Delete account. This is permanent and immediate. It erases your profile, session history, transactions, rewards and vouchers, and your unspent $OFF balance. Any stake locked in a room, duel or challenge is released first so that other players are not left waiting.

You do not need to email us, and we will not ask you why.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to your data, correction, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to contact@offcoin.app and we will answer within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa).

Children

OffCoin is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly create accounts for children. If you believe a child has an account, write to contact@offcoin.app and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
