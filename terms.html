What OffCoin is

OffCoin rewards you for time spent with your phone locked. You start a session, put the phone down, and if you stay off it until the session ends you earn $OFF — points inside the app.

$OFF is not money and not a cryptocurrency. It is a loyalty point that exists only inside OffCoin. It has no cash value, is not a financial instrument, is not a crypto-asset, is not stored on any blockchain, and cannot be bought, sold, exchanged for money, transferred between users, or withdrawn. We may change how $OFF is earned or what it can be exchanged for at any time. If your account is deleted — by you or by us — any $OFF balance ends with it and is not compensated.

The rewarded day

$OFF is earned only for time between 06:00 and midnight your local time. Time outside that window earns nothing. This exists for one reason: sleeping is not the behaviour this app is meant to reward, and without the limit an overnight session would pay more than a deliberate one during the day.

A session that would run past midnight is either shortened by the app or counted only up to midnight.

Stakes

Some modes let you stake $OFF — shared rooms, duels, teams, challenges. If you unlock your phone before the session ends, you lose the stake. This is the point of staking, and the app warns you before every one.

If a session cannot be settled because of a fault on our side, we return the stake. If it cannot be settled because you did not report a result, the outcome is decided from what the server can verify, which may be zero.
